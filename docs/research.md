# SENTINEL Research Infrastructure & Benchmark Suite

SENTINEL contains academic research evaluation pipelines:
- **Multi-Category Benchmark Suite**: 8 evaluation domains (`General QA`, `Reasoning`, `Instruction Following`, `Customer Support`, `Finance`, `RAG`, `Safety`, `Long Context`).
- **Baseline Comparison Study**: Compares Configurations A (Raw Model) through E (Full SENTINEL Platform).
- **Ablation Study Framework**: Quantifies accuracy loss when disabling diagnosis, vector embeddings, reranking, or verification gates.
- **Academic Paper Infrastructure**: Artifacts under `research/results/` and `research/paper/sentinel_paper.md`.
